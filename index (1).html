Our Mission

Communication is an essential part of care.

Words That Heal raises awareness about the importance of language access and effective communication in healthcare. Our mission is to educate future healthcare professionals about the challenges language barriers can create in patient care and the vital role of qualified medical interpreters.

We welcome students of all majors, backgrounds, and language abilities. Our goal is not to replace professional interpreters, but to help future clinicians recognize barriers to communication, understand when interpreters should be used, and become stronger advocates for their patients.

Signature Programming

Lost in Translation

An interactive healthcare communication simulation designed to show how easily meaning can be lost when patients and providers do not share a common language.

Participants work through realistic scenarios and then discuss the role of qualified interpreters, communication strategies, patient safety, and trust.
